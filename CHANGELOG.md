# ForeverUtils v2026.48.3

Target Finder can show a mob on the world map, and no longer leaves its marker on a mob another player has tagged.

## New

- **Show on the map.** Each mob in the `/tf` window has a map button. Click it to open the world map on the zone where that mob spawns, with its spawn points pinned. Click it again to clear them. They are where the mob is known to spawn, not where it is right now. Needs QuestieDB.
- **Unmark tagged mobs.** When the macro lands on a mob another player has tagged, the raid marker comes straight back off it, so the marker only stays on mobs you can get credit for. Mobs tagged by your own group keep it. Untick **Unmark tagged** in the `/tf` window to turn it off.

## Good to know

- **The macro can't skip tagged mobs.** WoW gives macros no way to tell, and addons can't change your target, so you still have to move to another one yourself.
