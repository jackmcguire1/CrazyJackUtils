# CrazyJackUtils v2026.48.5

ForeverUtils is now called CrazyJackUtils. Nothing else about it has changed.

## Changed

- **New name.** The addon shows as **CrazyJackUtils** in your addon list, the options and chat.
- **`/fu` and `/tf` still work,** and so do the `FUFind` macros on your action bars. `/cju` and `/crazyjackutils` also open the options; `/foreverutils` is gone.

## If you had ForeverUtils installed

- **Your settings start fresh.** The game saves settings under the addon's name, so your find list, history and options reset once.
- **Key bindings need setting again,** under Key Bindings → AddOns → CrazyJackUtils.
- **Delete the old `ForeverUtils` folder** from `Interface/AddOns` if you installed by hand, or both will load.
